## 1. What they are

- Human-built war machines on the castle wall, modified with chains, gears, cranks and buttons so the giant can run them.
- A separate category from giant tools:
  - **Castle machines:** powerful, fixed to the wall, cover a cone in front of them, depend on supply carts.
  - **Giant tools:** handheld, aim anywhere, powered by food.

## 2. What was discussed

**Placement**
- Machines sit on the wall top, inside comfortable hand reach.
- The player operates them directly on the machine.
- Dropped earlier: a separate control ledge with ropes to each machine, terraces, and pie-slice wedge modules. These came from an older layout where the wall was too far and too tall.

**Aiming**
- Each machine covers a cone (about 40 to 60 degrees) in front of it.
- Direction: grab the machine and twist it within its cone.
- Range: how hard you pull or how much you crank.
- A dotted landing marker shows where it will hit, with mild auto-aim toward the nearest group.

**Supply**
- Workers drag carts from stores in the courtyard, up ramps on the inside of the wall, to the machines.
- Shown in the mockup (`maps/castle-3d.html`): a cannonball cart to the cannon and an arrow cart to the arrow volley.
- Intruders in the courtyard make workers panic and stop supply.

**Loadout**
- Suggested: 2 or 3 machines per battle, chosen before the battle. Not decided.

## 3. Machine ideas

| Machine | Hand action | Payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Catapult | Drop a boulder in the bucket, pull the arm, release | Big arc, area splash |
| Arrow volley | Crank to wind, press a button | A cloud of arrows, good on groups |
| Cannon | Load, light the fuse | Big explosion |
| Ballista | Pull the string back, release | One bolt pierces a line |
| Boiling cauldron | Tip it over the wall edge | Clears climbers |

Other ideas mentioned: fireworks rack, log roller, bellows or fan, crank-powered rapid crossbow.

## 4. Open problems

- **No enemy needs them.** The battering ram and the brute were removed, so no enemy has machines as its best answer.
- **Role overlap.** Giant tools already cover the active, high-impact moments, so it is unclear what machines add.
- **Scope.** Each machine needs its own mechanic, aiming, supply and juice. About 3 weeks remain before the deadline.

## 5. Proposal to test after the playtest (not decided)

- **Machines fire on their own** at enemies in their cone, as long as carts keep them supplied.
- **The player's interaction is an optional boost:** crank to fire faster, or pull to release one big charged shot.
- Why: supports the calm phases (the castle defends itself while you watch), gives workers and carts a purpose, and does not need a counter enemy.
- Build only 1 or 2 (for example arrow volley and catapult).
- Alternative: cut machines entirely. Giant tools, hands and the army may already be a complete game.
